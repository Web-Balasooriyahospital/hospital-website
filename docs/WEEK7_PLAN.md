## What changed on the home page
It had been the original first-week placeholder: a hero and three generic
"why choose us" cards. It now carries:

- An **emergency bar above the header** with the ETU and ambulance numbers
  as `tel:` links. Those numbers were previously three clicks away on the
  contact page, which is the wrong place for them.
- Quick links to the five things people actually arrive looking for.
- Department and specialist teasers that funnel into the full listings.

  certifications) and the hospital's award record.
- A visit-us block with the real address, hotlines, and email.

## Note on the groundwork task
The worksheet asks for providers with a **BAA**. That is a HIPAA instrument,
and HIPAA does not apply to a hospital operating solely in Sri Lanka. The
research records this and recommends requiring a **PDPA-compliant Data
Processing Agreement** instead, treating BAA availability only as a
shortlist signal. Worth confirming with legal.
