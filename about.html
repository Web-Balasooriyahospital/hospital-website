About Balasooriya Hospital

Our Story

Established in 2009, Balasooriya Hospital (Pvt) Ltd is a leading private healthcare facility in Puttalam, in Sri Lanka's North Western Province. We provide medical and surgical services 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, backed by modern technology and an experienced clinical team.

Our Vision

To be the Most Reliable Private Healthcare Organization in Sri Lanka.

Our Mission

Delivering a modern technology-based, quality and efficient total healthcare solution through our experienced staff.
